## 💫 About Me

- 🎓 **Computer Science** student at **Egypt-Japan University of Science and Technology (E-JUST)**
- 🧠 Building **[NeuroMove](https://github.com/Ahmedvini/NeuroMove)** — an FPGA-accelerated EEG-based Brain-Computer Interface for stroke rehabilitation
- 🔬 Working at the intersection of **FPGA acceleration, embedded systems, AI, and biosignal processing**
- 🔧 Embedded Systems Engineer & Instructor at **Mycrosys E-JUST**
- 🏛️ Treasurer, **IEEE E-JUST RAS Student Branch Chapter** | IEEE Member
- 📄 Co-author of research on FPGA-accelerated EEG decoding and biomaterial FEM verification
- 🚀 Open to opportunities in **Embedded Systems, FPGA, Edge AI, Computer Architecture, and Applied ML**
- ⚡ Fun fact: I've debugged everything from deep-learning pipelines to Vivado bitstream generation in the same week
